Study design

How the elevated mPAP classification target was assembled

01

Identify cohort

7,815 ICU patients with at least one valid pulmonary artery pressure mean reading (mPAP 5–100 mmHg).

02

Define elevated mPAP label

Median mPAP greater than 20 mmHg in the 24-hour window after index time. Index is the first valid reading.

03

Build predictors

Demographic, admission, and history features plus the last available laboratory results from the preceding 72 hours.

04

Compare models

Model A uses 55 features. Model B adds cardiac output (56 features), available in 84.2% of the cohort.
